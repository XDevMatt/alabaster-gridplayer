# Audio

For volume and mute, see [Volume](volume.md). Everything else is on the
right-click menu under **Audio**, per cell, and **[ALL] > Audio**.

## Audio delay (`K` / `J`)

When sound and picture are out of step:

| Do | This cell | Every cell |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Sound later | `K` | `Shift+K` |
| Sound earlier | `J` | `Shift+J` |
| Reset | **Audio > Audio Delay > Reset** | **[ALL] > Audio > Audio Delay > Reset** |
| Exact value | **Audio > Audio Delay: …** | **[ALL] > Audio > Audio Delay > Set Delay** |

Each press moves the delay by 50 ms, up to 60 seconds either way. The delay
is saved with the playlist.

## Audio track

**Audio > Audio Track** picks a track. The New Videos default (Settings,
Defaults, New Videos, Audio, **Audio track**) can be the file's default, a
**Preferred Language** (with a list of languages, best first), or **Disable
Audio** (no audio decoded at all, which isn't the same as muted).

### Separate audio files

Turn on **Detect external audio files** (New Videos, Audio) and audio files
next to a video with the same name are offered in **Audio Track**. They're
opened only when you pick one.

## Audio mode

**Audio > Audio Mode**: Original, Stereo, Reverse Stereo, Left, Right, Dolby
Surround, Headphones, Mono.

## Audio device

**Audio > Audio Device** sends one cell's sound to another output, for
example headphones for one cell and speakers for the rest.

## Audio files

Audio files play in a cell like videos, with an audio icon in place of a
picture. A dropped audio file always opens. A dropped **folder** includes
audio only when its **Files to Play** is **All media**; see [Dropping folders](folder-drop.md).
